RESEARCH DATA POLICY

1. Every text record must identify its source, edition or witness, language, licence, and transcription type.
2. An edited text must never be labelled as a direct manuscript transcription.
3. A manuscript must not be described as containing a passage unless that witness actually preserves the passage.
4. Dead Sea Scrolls are Hebrew/Aramaic witnesses. They are not witnesses to the Apostolic Writings.
5. Reconstructed, damaged, supplied, or uncertain readings must be marked explicitly.
6. Lost works may be catalogued from surviving references, but their missing contents must never be fabricated.
7. Translation output must identify its source or engine, date, and human-review status.
8. Public-domain or compatible licensed data only. Preserve attribution and licence notices in the app.
9. The present bundled Hebrew and Greek texts are research starting editions, not a complete manuscript archive or critical apparatus.

10. TEXT-FIRST DEVELOPMENT PRIORITY
    Complete the searchable text corpus before undertaking manuscript-photo or facsimile integration.
    Prioritize original-language transcriptions, manuscript identifiers, passage coverage, fragment/column/line coordinates, editorial marks, and source/licence metadata.
    Build exact-word and exact-phrase search over the integrated text, with witness filters and clear separation of manuscript text, editorial reconstruction, and comparison witnesses.
    Photographs and facsimiles are deferred until a later app update after the text archive is substantially complete. Their absence must not block text ingestion, verification, indexing, or search.
    Do not describe the text archive as complete until coverage has been inventoried and gaps, unavailable texts, and unverified readings are explicitly recorded.
